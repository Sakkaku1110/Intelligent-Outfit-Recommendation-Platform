# ClothesAI Logo Direction

Final recommendation: **A. Closet Spark**.

| 方案 | 核心符号 | 优点 | 风险 | 评分 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A. Closet Spark | 双门衣柜 + AI 星标 | 最清爽，衣柜语义明确，绿色星标表达 AI 智能推荐，误读风险最低 | AI 含义比芯片更抽象，但更适合 App 图标 | 96 |
| B. Fit Loop | C 字母 + 穿搭闭环 | 品牌感更强，适合动效 | 衣柜语义稍弱 | 84 |
| C. Neural Hanger | 电路衣架 | AI 感明显 | 小尺寸细节偏复杂 | 79 |
| D. Closet Check | 衣柜 + 勾选 | 可靠、直观 | 更像任务清单或库存工具 | 76 |

Files:

- `clothesai-logo-mark.svg`: square app icon / favicon / compact header icon.
- `clothesai-logo-final.svg`: horizontal lockup for docs, posters, and presentation cover.
- `logo-concepts.svg`: comparison board for deciding the final direction.
